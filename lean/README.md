# Lean 4 Formalization of Defect Integrability Proofs

This directory contains a self-contained Lean 4 (`v4.35.0`) formalization ([`DefectIntegrability.lean`](DefectIntegrability.lean)) of the algebraic, symplectic, and differential-algebraic skeleton of the lemmas, propositions, and theorems in [`defect-integrability/defect_integrability_paper.md`](../defect-integrability/defect_integrability_paper.md) (*"Momentum-Conserving Defects as Spatial Hamilton–Jacobi Correspondences and the Corrigan–Zambon Integrability Conjecture"*) and [`defect-integrability/corrigan_zambon_conjecture_report.md`](../defect-integrability/corrigan_zambon_conjecture_report.md).

It uses only core Lean 4 (no external library dependencies) so that `lake build` compiles from scratch in a few seconds with zero `sorry` placeholders and zero custom axioms.

## Scope and Design of the Formalization

To keep the formalization self-contained in core Lean 4 while faithfully verifying the non-trivial algebraic, symplectic, and differential-algebraic steps of the paper:
- **Commuting Leibniz derivations (`SpaceTimeDerivations`):** Spacetime derivatives `(dt, dx)` are axiomatized as commuting additive derivations satisfying the **Leibniz product rule** `dt (a * b) = dt a * b + a * dt b` and `dx (a * b) = dx a * b + a * dx b` (from which `dt 0 = 0`, `dt 1 = 0`, and constant-multiple linearity `dt c = 0 → dt (c * a) = c * dt a` follow as theorems).
- **Strong Lagrangian correspondences (`IsLagrangianSubspace`) and Schur-complement transversality (`schur_complement_transversality`):** Symplectic and linear-algebraic theorems in Section 3 are proved over arbitrary types `U_minus, U_plus` equipped with non-degenerate bilinear pairings, establishing both **isotropy and coisotropy** ($T\Gamma_d = (T\Gamma_d)^\perp$) of the correspondence generated by $S_d$ and proving both `transverse_right` and `transverse_left` from invertibility of the Schur-complement mixed Hessians $\widetilde S_{\Phi\Psi}, \widetilde S_{\Psi\Phi}$.
- **Explicit polynomial Taylor expansions (`quadTaylor`) and recursive symbol extraction:** In Section 4, the $O(\epsilon^{-1})$, $O(\epsilon^0)$, and $O(\epsilon^1)$ coefficients of $\mathcal E_d(\epsilon)$ are extracted by applying an explicit quadratic Taylor polynomial function `quadTaylor` to the perturbation $\delta = \epsilon\Psi_1 + \epsilon^2\Psi_2$, and the principal symbol formula (4.11) is derived by induction from the inhomogeneous recursion (4.10).
- **Analytic/functional-analytic hypotheses:** Infinite-dimensional PDE analysis, functional-analytic domain issues, and the equal-time Hamiltonian generator and Bianchi permutability properties in Theorem 2(3) are represented as explicit hypotheses of the corresponding theorems, matching their conditional formulation in the paper.
